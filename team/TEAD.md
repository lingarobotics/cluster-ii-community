# Team

## Overview

Cluster II was a student-driven academic initiative established to encourage collaboration, knowledge sharing, and peer learning among students from multiple engineering disciplines.

The initiative functioned through faculty guidance and student leadership, enabling students to organize technical sessions, workshops, and community-driven learning activities.

## Responsibilities

Student representatives collectively contributed to:

- Organizing technical sessions and workshops.
- Coordinating communication between students and faculty.
- Promoting peer-driven learning initiatives.
- Identifying educational resources and learning opportunities.
- Encouraging students to learn beyond the prescribed curriculum.
- Documenting community activities and preserving their outcomes.

---

## Guiding Principle

> **For the students, by the students.**

Cluster II was built on the belief that meaningful learning grows when students actively share knowledge, experiences, and opportunities with one another, while being supported by faculty mentors.
