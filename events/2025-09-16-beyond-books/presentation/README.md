# Presentation

## Overview

This presentation was prepared specifically for the **Beyond Books: A Way of Learning** session conducted for first-year engineering students on **16 September 2025**.

Rather than functioning as a traditional slide deck, it was designed to guide discussions, encourage critical thinking, and challenge common assumptions about engineering education.

---

## Target Audience

- First-Year Engineering Students

---

## Learning Objectives

The presentation aimed to help students:

- Understand engineering beyond examinations and degree completion.
- Differentiate between studying and learning.
- Think critically about career planning from the first year.
- Explore opportunities such as Minor and Honors Degrees.
- Discover trusted free learning platforms and certifications.
- Develop the habit of continuous self-learning.

---

## Topics Covered

- What is Engineering?
- Who is an Engineer?
- Engineering Mindset
- Learning Beyond the Curriculum
- Product-Based vs. Service-Based Careers
- Minor and Honors Degrees
- Skills vs. Certificates
- Learning at No Cost
- Building Identity During College

---

## About the Presentation

The presentation was intentionally designed to support interaction rather than one-way delivery.

Many slides were discussion-driven and encouraged students to question, reflect, and contribute their own perspectives instead of passively consuming information.

---

## Reusability

Future student mentors and community members are encouraged to adapt this presentation for similar awareness sessions while preserving its objective of promoting practical engineering, continuous learning, and responsible knowledge sharing.
