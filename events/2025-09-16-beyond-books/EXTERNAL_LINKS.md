## Related Community Initiative

This event introduced ideas that later became recurring themes in future Cluster II sessions, including:

- Engineering mindset
- Self-driven learning
- Practical engineering
- Learning beyond the curriculum
- Career awareness
- Open knowledge sharing

These ideas were further expanded in subsequent workshops and mentoring sessions conducted through the community.
